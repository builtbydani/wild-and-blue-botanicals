The slow way

Small batches, slow infusions

My products feature handcrafted, infused oils: a simple, old-fashioned technique performed by hand for every batch, yielding nourishing, safe, and effective skin treatments.

01

Harvest & dry

Flowers, leaves, and roots are grown, harvested, or foraged from my home garden and the fields around Duluth whenever possible.

02

Steep for weeks

The dried botanicals soak in carrier oil for several weeks, letting their compounds transfer naturally into golden oils.

03

Blend by hand

Each product is formulated, blended, tested, and refined in small batches with carefully selected ingredients.
